Privacy Policy

LineLeaf · Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how LineLeaf ("LineLeaf", "the App", "we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use the App.

LineLeaf is an offline text-cleaning and formatting utility. The App is designed to process text locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives or stores the text you enter or process in LineLeaf.

2. Information the App processes

To provide its features, LineLeaf may process text that you:

  • type or paste into the editor;
  • explicitly send to LineLeaf through Android's share interface; or
  • select in another app and explicitly open with Android's text-processing action.

The text is processed locally on your device. LineLeaf does not transmit this text to the developer or to a LineLeaf server. The text may contain personal information if you choose to enter or share such information.

3. Clipboard access

LineLeaf reads clipboard text only after you tap the Paste action. The App does not continuously monitor the clipboard.

When you tap Copy, or choose the copy option for processed text, LineLeaf places the requested text on the device's system clipboard. Clipboard availability and retention are controlled by Android and your device settings. Other apps may be able to access clipboard content according to Android's rules and your device's behavior.

4. Sharing text with other apps

If you choose Share in LineLeaf, Android displays its system share interface so you can select another app or recipient. LineLeaf transfers the text you chose to the destination you select. If you use Android's selected-text processing feature, LineLeaf returns the processed text to the requesting app when you choose the relevant action.

These transfers happen at your direction. The destination app or service is operated by its own provider and is subject to that provider's privacy policy and practices. LineLeaf does not control how a destination app uses information after you send it there.

5. Information we collect and share

In the version described by this Policy, LineLeaf does not collect or upload the text you process, does not create user accounts, and does not send personal data to developer-operated servers. The App does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs.

LineLeaf does not sell your personal information. We do not share information with the developer or third parties for advertising or analytics purposes. Text is transferred to another app only when you explicitly choose an Android sharing or text-processing action, as described above.

6. Storage, retention and deletion

LineLeaf does not save text drafts or processing history to its own persistent app storage. Text is held temporarily in the App while you use it. Android may temporarily preserve app state to restore an activity after a system-managed recreation. This is managed by Android on your device, not by a LineLeaf server.

If you copy text, it remains on the system clipboard according to Android and your device's clipboard behavior. You can replace it by copying other content or use your device's clipboard controls where available. If you share text with another app, manage or delete that content through the destination app.

7. Permissions and network access

The current LineLeaf build does not request Android runtime permissions and does not declare the Android Internet permission. Its text-cleaning features operate on-device.

8. Children's privacy

LineLeaf is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Because the App does not collect or upload user text, we do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the App.

9. Security

Because text processing takes place locally and the App does not upload text to a LineLeaf server, the App does not maintain a remote database of your text. You should still protect your device with appropriate screen-lock and account-security settings, and take care when copying or sharing sensitive text.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features or data practices change. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If a future version introduces data collection, network transmission, analytics, advertising, or additional third-party services, this Policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before those practices are introduced.
